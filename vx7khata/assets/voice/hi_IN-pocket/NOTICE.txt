Changes by Scribe Animator / OHO Tech, 2026-10-21:
Exported with the official pocket-tts 3.1.0 runtime to four functional ONNX graphs.
Transformer projection/feed-forward weights use symmetric per-output-channel signed INT8 storage with DequantizeLinear; activations, text conditioner, flow sampler and Mimi decoder remain floating point.
Recurrent cache updates are functional. This derivative is not an official Indus release or a community dynamic INT8 model.
No publisher Python normalizer or voice-cloning encoder is shipped or executed. User words are not automatically substituted.
